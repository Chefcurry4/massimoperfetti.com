I’m a mechanical engineer working where fluid dynamics meets machine learning. These days I’m in San Francisco building Antemyth with three friends from MIT. AI is running out of power faster than it’s running out of chips, and plenty of buildings already have spare power they never use. So we put GPU boxes there and switch them on in weeks, not years.

Before this I was at MIT’s van Rees Lab teaching neural networks how fluids move, after studying at TU Delft, EPFL and Politecnico di Torino. Outside of work I’m usually watching the NBA, playing chess or reading.
