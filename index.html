Mechanical engineer working at the intersection of fluid dynamics and machine learning.

I’m a visiting researcher at MIT’s van Rees Lab, working on AI for computational fluid dynamics. I studied at MIT and TU Delft (MSc) and at EPFL and Politecnico di Torino (BSc), and interned at Novelis Sierre on multiphase, multi-physics flows.

I’m also Co-Founder & CTO of Dual Energy, a Swiss startup developing agrivoltaics technology.

Outside the lab: NBA, chess, logic games, reading, and building things.
